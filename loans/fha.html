What Is an FHA Loan?

An FHA loan is a mortgage insured by the Federal Housing Administration (FHA), a part of the U.S. Department of Housing and Urban Development. Here at Efinity Mortgage, we follow FHA's guidelines, offering more flexibility to help our clients qualify, which is part of why FHA loans are popular with first-time buyers.

Who FHA Loans Are Often a Good Fit For

  • First-time homebuyers
  • Buyers with a shorter or less established credit history
  • Buyers looking for more flexible down payment options
  • Buyers who want to use gift funds or down payment assistance alongside their loan

Down Payment & Mortgage Insurance

FHA loans generally allow for a lower down payment than many other loan types, and specific down payment requirements can vary based on your credit profile and current guidelines, so it's worth reviewing your exact numbers together.

FHA loans also require mortgage insurance, which adds to your monthly payment and is separate from your loan's interest rate.

FHA Loans & Down Payment Assistance

FHA loans can often be paired with down payment assistance programs, depending on your eligibility and the specific program.

Is an FHA Loan Right for You?

Every borrower's situation is different. Reviewing your income, credit, and goals together is the best way to know whether an FHA loan, or another program, fits what you're trying to do.

Common Questions

Do I need perfect credit for an FHA loan?

Not necessarily. With a credit score of 580 or higher, you may qualify with as little as 3.5% down. With a score between 500 and 579, FHA guidelines generally require at least 10% down. It's worth reviewing your specific credit situation together to confirm what applies to you.

Can I use an FHA loan for any type of home?

FHA loans are generally used for primary residences that meet FHA property standards. Some multi-unit properties may also qualify.

Can I combine an FHA loan with down payment assistance?

In many cases, yes, depending on the specific program and your eligibility.

What's the difference between an FHA loan and a Conventional loan?

FHA loans are government-insured with more flexible qualifying guidelines. Conventional loans aren't government-insured and follow different guidelines. Learn More About Conventional Loans
