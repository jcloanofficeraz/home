VA Loans in Arizona & Texas

A loan benefit for eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and certain military spouses, with $0 down payment and no monthly mortgage insurance.

What Is a VA Loan?

A VA loan is a mortgage guaranteed by the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs. Here at Efinity Mortgage, we help eligible veterans, active-duty service members, and certain surviving spouses use this benefit to buy a home with $0 down and no monthly mortgage insurance.

Who VA Loans Are Often a Good Fit For

  • Veterans, active-duty service members, and members of the National Guard or Reserves who meet service requirements
  • Certain surviving spouses of veterans
  • Buyers who want to purchase with no down payment
  • Buyers who want to avoid monthly mortgage insurance

Down Payment & the VA Funding Fee

VA loans don't require a down payment or monthly mortgage insurance for eligible borrowers. Instead, most VA loans include a one-time funding fee, which for first-time use is 2.15% with no down payment, dropping to 1.5% with at least 5% down, and 1.25% with at least 10% down.

Veterans receiving VA disability compensation, along with certain surviving spouses and Purple Heart recipients, are generally exempt from this fee altogether.

Eligibility & the Certificate of Eligibility (COE)

To use a VA loan, you'll need a Certificate of Eligibility (COE) confirming your service meets VA requirements. We can help you request your COE as part of the process.

Is a VA Loan Right for You?

Every borrower's situation is different. Reviewing your service history, eligibility, and goals together is the best way to know whether a VA loan fits what you're trying to do.

Common Questions

Do I need a down payment for a VA loan?

Generally no. Eligible borrowers can often purchase with $0 down.

What credit score do I need for a VA loan?

The VA itself doesn't set a minimum credit score. Here at Efinity Mortgage, we look at your full financial picture following VA guidelines, so it's worth reviewing your specific situation together to see what you may qualify for.

Do I have to pay the VA funding fee?

Most borrowers do, though veterans receiving VA disability compensation and certain surviving spouses are generally exempt.

Can I use a VA loan more than once?

Yes, eligible borrowers can use their VA loan benefit multiple times, though the funding fee is generally higher for subsequent use.
