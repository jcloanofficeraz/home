Bilingual Service, Start to Finish

Buying a home involves a lot of paperwork and unfamiliar terms, and that can be even harder to navigate in a second language. I work with clients in both English and Spanish so you can ask questions and go through the process in whichever language you're most comfortable with.

What I Can Help With

  • Explaining your loan options in plain language, including FHA, conventional, VA, USDA, construction, and ITIN home loans
  • Reviewing down payment assistance programs that may fit your situation
  • Answering questions at any stage, whether you're just starting to explore or ready to make an offer
  • Working alongside your real estate agent to keep things moving smoothly
  • A clear walkthrough of your Loan Estimate. Once you're under contract, I review your Loan Estimate with you in person, by phone, or by video call, so you understand exactly what you're paying and the terms of your loan, including any assistance program you're using.
  • Direct access to me and my processing team. I also work closely with our in-house underwriting team, which means faster answers and fewer delays for you throughout the process.
