Buckeye Night Market

September 11, 2026 · Downtown Buckeye, AZ

View photos & video (4)

I had a great time participating in the Buckeye Night Market. The energy was excellent, I made new connections, and I got to talk with so many people from the community. The staff were attentive and friendly the whole night, and the music kept everyone moving if you're the dancing type. First-Time Homebuyer Class

August 1, 2026 · Efinity Mortgage Office, Phoenix, AZ

View photos & video (4)

Our first-time homebuyer seminar was a full experience: educational, relaxed, and genuinely valuable for every attendee. Throughout the session, our guests learned step by step how the home buying process works, from preapproval to closing, with real examples and total clarity. They also enjoyed a welcoming atmosphere with fresh coffee from Latte Amor Cafe and even chair massages from Massage Rested, which made the day even more special. Without a doubt, an event that marked the beginning of many new stories.
